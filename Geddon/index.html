DETAILS THAT EARN
THEIR PLACE.

01 / REFLECTIVE HOOD CROWN

The proposed build. Final materials, fit and performance will be signed off through sample development and testing.

01 / REFLECTIVE HOOD CROWN

A reflective panel at the top of the hood, positioned to help improve visibility when you're at the surface.

02 / HIGH ON THE STERNUM

A shield-shaped 7 mm loading pad with a tone-on-tone G. A line-cutter pocket sits at the top.

03 / WITHIN REACH

Left-forearm knife sleeve, right-upper-arm beacon pocket and an inner key pocket. Purposeful storage close to hand.

04 / STAYS WITH YOU

Silicone weight-belt grip, two hip carabiner loops, smoothskin seals and a double-clip beavertail.

05 / OPEN-CELL INSIDE

Specified in Yamamoto 39 limestone neoprene, with glued and blindstitched seams, knee and elbow reinforcement, and panel stitching on show.
