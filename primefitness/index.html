The approach

Progress has principles.
Make them your routine.

A clear plan for the work, the fuel and the recovery. Build habits you can return to, week after week.

01 / TRAIN WITH PURPOSE

Build on your last session.

Follow structured strength sessions. Log your sets, reps and loads, and use progressive overload to build gradually as you’re ready.

02 / FUEL THE WORK

Make nutrition practical.

Keep track of what you eat. Put protein, varied nutrient-dense foods and fuel for training at the centre of your daily routine.

03 / KEEP SHOWING UP

Consistency includes recovery.

Balance training with rest. Start at an appropriate level, pay attention to how you feel and build a routine you can sustain.
